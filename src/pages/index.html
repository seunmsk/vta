Why a second citizenship

Freedom you can hand down to your children

Travel without the queue

Visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 145+ destinations, including the Schengen area and Singapore.

A Plan B for your family

Citizenship is for life and passes to future generations, giving you security no matter what changes at home.

No need to relocate

Keep your life where it is. Most programmes have no residency requirement and no interview.

Global business mobility

Open companies and bank accounts abroad more easily, and in Grenada’s case, pursue the US E-2 visa.

Education options

Live, work and study across CARICOM, with more options for your children’s schooling.

Tax-friendly jurisdictions

Caribbean CBI countries do not tax worldwide income of non-residents. Take advice on your own situation.
