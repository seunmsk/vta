01 · What is CBI

Citizenship, granted for investing in a nation's economy

Citizenship by Investment is a legal programme, created by a country's own laws, that grants full citizenship to people who make an approved economic contribution.

The money funds national development: infrastructure, housing, health and recovery from natural disasters. In return, you and your family receive citizenship for life, which passes to your children.

Route A
Government fund contribution

A one-time, non-refundable payment to a national development fund. The simplest and usually cheapest route, from $200,000.

Route B
Approved real estate

Buy into a government-approved development, such as a hotel or resort. You own an asset you can sell after the holding period, from $200,000.

Four decades of Caribbean CBI
1984

St Kitts & Nevis launches the world’s first CBI programme.

1993

Dominica follows with its Economic Citizenship Programme.

2013

Antigua & Barbuda and Grenada open their programmes.

2015

Saint Lucia launches the newest Caribbean programme.

2024

All five agree a common minimum investment of $200,000.

2026

A regional regulator begins overseeing all five programmes.
