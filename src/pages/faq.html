Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what clients ask first

The basicsIs Citizenship by Investment legal?
Yes. Each programme is created by the country’s own laws and run by its government Citizenship by Investment Unit. Applications are submitted through licensed agents.
The basicsWill I have to give up my current citizenship?
All five Caribbean programmes allow dual citizenship. Most countries, including Nigeria, the UK and the US, also let you keep your existing nationality. We confirm your home country’s rules at the start.
The basicsWhich passport is best for me?
It depends on what matters most. St Kitts & Nevis is the strongest and fastest, Dominica is the most affordable, Antigua suits large families, and Grenada is the only one with a route to the US E-2 visa.
FamilyCan I include my family?
Yes. Every programme lets you add your spouse and dependent children, and most allow dependent parents. Some also allow siblings. Age limits differ by country.
FamilyWill my future children be citizens too?
Yes. Citizenship passes by descent, so children born after you become a citizen can apply as well.
CostsHow much does it cost in total?
Programmes start at $200,000 for a single applicant. On top of the investment there are government processing, due-diligence and passport fees, plus our professional fee. We give you one written quote before you commit.
CostsWhen do I pay the investment?
Only after the government has approved your application in principle. You pay the government or approved developer directly, never through us.
CostsDo I get my money back?
Fund contributions are non-refundable. Real estate is an asset you own and can sell after the required holding period. Saint Lucia’s bond option is returned after the holding period.
ProcessHow long does it take?
Typically 4 to 9 months from submission. St Kitts & Nevis is usually fastest. Preparing documents adds 2 to 6 weeks at the start.
ProcessDo I need to travel or attend an interview?
No interview or language test is required, and the whole application can be done from wherever you live. Antigua & Barbuda asks for a 5-day visit within the first five years.
ProcessWhat could get my application refused?
Usually a criminal record, a previous visa refusal that wasn’t disclosed, or funds whose source can’t be proven. We review your profile at the start so there are no surprises.
Living & travelDo I have to live in the country?
No. Apart from Antigua’s short visit, there is no residency requirement. You can keep living and working where you are.
Living & travelWhich countries can I visit visa-free?
Between about 145 and 155 destinations depending on the passport, including the Schengen area and Singapore. The exact list differs by country, so ask us for the one you’re considering.
Living & travelWill I pay tax there?
The Caribbean CBI countries do not tax the worldwide income of non-residents. Your tax position at home and elsewhere depends on your own circumstances, so take independent advice.
